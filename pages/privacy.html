Privacy Policy

Draft. Please review this text and adjust it before the site goes live.

What we collect

When you ask for a free demo class we collect your name, WhatsApp number and the course you are interested in. When you register for a course we collect the details you enter in the form: your name, father's name, date of birth, gender, nationality, mobile number, email, address, the course you chose, and the payment screenshot you upload.

How we use it

We use this information to confirm your seat, contact you about your classes, and keep our student records. We do not sell your information.

Where it is kept

Registrations are stored in a private Google Sheets file and Google Drive folder that only ACE Training Lab staff can open.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time by emailing infoacetraininglab@gmail.com.
